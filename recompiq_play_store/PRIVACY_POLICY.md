# Privacy Policy for RecompIQ (`com.recompiq.app`)

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026

## 1. Local-First Data Storage (Zero Cloud Collection)
RecompIQ stores all biometric logs (weight, body measurements, sleep, resting heart rate), strength workout sets, and nutrition/macro entries **100% locally on your device** using encrypted Android WebView local storage (`localStorage`).

- **No External Servers:** RecompIQ does not transmit, upload, or sync your personal health or fitness data to any external server.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers:** RecompIQ contains zero advertising SDKs, third-party trackers, or telemetry scripts.

## 2. User Data Control, Export & Deletion
You retain complete ownership of your data at all times:
- **Export:** You can export your full history as a `.csv` spreadsheet or `.json` backup file directly to your device via the in-app **Data** menu.
- **Deletion:** You can permanently erase all stored data at any time using the **Reset to Clean Slate** button inside the app or by clearing app storage in Android Settings.

## 3. Contact
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact: `support@recompiq.example.com`
